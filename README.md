# ⚡ Electric Vehicle Analysis | Tableau Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Electric Vehicle Analysis** project is an interactive Tableau data visualization project designed to analyze the growth, distribution, and characteristics of electric vehicles.

The dashboard transforms raw electric vehicle data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand EV adoption trends, leading manufacturers, vehicle types, model popularity, electric range, and geographic distribution.

This project demonstrates my ability to perform **data analysis, KPI development, dashboard design, and data storytelling using Tableau**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze the overall adoption of electric vehicles.
* Identify the most popular EV manufacturers and models.
* Compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs).
* Analyze EV distribution across different locations.
* Study trends based on vehicle model year.
* Evaluate electric range across vehicles.
* Build an interactive dashboard for easy exploration and decision-making.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard focuses on important EV metrics such as:

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 🚘 Total Vehicles by Model Year

Analyzes how electric vehicle adoption has changed over time and highlights years with significant growth.

### 🗺️ Total Vehicles by State

Shows the geographic distribution of electric vehicles and helps identify regions with higher EV adoption.

### 🏭 Top Vehicle Manufacturers

Compares EV manufacturers based on the number of registered vehicles and identifies the leading brands in the market.

### 🚗 Vehicle Model Analysis

Highlights the most popular electric vehicle models based on total vehicle count.

### 🔋 BEV vs PHEV Analysis

Compares **Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs)** and **Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)** to understand their contribution to the overall EV market.

### 📏 Electric Range Analysis

Evaluates the electric driving range of vehicles to understand differences across EV models and manufacturers.
